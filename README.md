# Rental Deal Calculator

A free calculator for rental property investors. Enter a property's price, loan terms, rent and expenses to see:

- Monthly and annual cash flow
- Cap rate and cash-on-cash return
- Total cash needed to buy
- Where the rent goes, expense by expense
- The maximum price to offer to hit a target monthly profit

## Install it as an app

**iPhone / iPad:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
**Android:** open the link in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Install app** (or Add to Home screen).
**Windows / Mac:** open the link in Chrome or Edge and click the **Install** icon in the address bar.

After the first visit it works offline. Your numbers are saved only on your own device.

*Estimates only, not financial advice.*
